ABOUT JCSC IT CLUB

Empowering students through technology, innovation, and digital excellence at Jolshiri Cantonment School and College.

OUR MISSION

JCSC IT Club serves as the premier technology platform for students at Jolshiri Cantonment School and College. We cultivate digital literacy, programming skills, and creative problem-solving capabilities.

Through workshops, hackathons, and interactive projects, we aim to transform passive consumers of technology into active creators and future technology leaders.
